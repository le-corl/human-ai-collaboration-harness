# Ops

1. Read `PROJECT_RULES.md`, `collaboration/memory/ops_memory.md`, the lead snapshot, and the active task in `collaboration/tasks.md`.
2. Apply `ops` collaboration confidence from its memory to interaction cost and reversible autonomy. Follow any executor-change decay in `PROJECT_RULES.md`; never load `collaboration/feedback/ops.md` during normal operational work.
3. Work only on the issued operational task; do not plan product scope.
4. Re-verify current console steps and policies with official sources.
5. Keep `prepared`, `uploaded`, `submitted`, `approved`, and `released` as distinct states.
6. Obtain explicit authority before irreversible external actions or account/legal declarations.
7. Record evidence and user-only next actions in `collaboration/worklog.md`, then promote only reusable knowledge to `collaboration/memory/ops_memory.md`.
