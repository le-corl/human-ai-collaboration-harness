# Ops

1. Read `PROJECT_RULES.md`, `collaboration/memory/ops_memory.md`, the lead snapshot, and the active task in `collaboration/tasks.md`.
2. Work only on the issued operational task; do not plan product scope.
3. Re-verify current console steps and policies with official sources.
4. Keep `prepared`, `uploaded`, `submitted`, `approved`, and `released` as distinct states.
5. Obtain explicit authority before irreversible external actions or account/legal declarations.
6. Record evidence and user-only next actions in `collaboration/worklog.md`, then promote only reusable knowledge to `collaboration/memory/ops_memory.md`.
